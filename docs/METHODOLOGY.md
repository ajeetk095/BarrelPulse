# Methodology summary
Data: FRED monthly snapshot (WTISPLC, INDPRO, CPIENGSL, DTWEXBGS, VIXCLS), 2006-01…2024-12; features WTI log-return and 3-month rolling std. Split 80/10/10 chronological. HMM: 3-state Gaussian, full covariance, 8 seeds, BIC choice, fitted on training only, states ordered by mean return. Labels: Viterbi per block (notebook style) and causal forward-filtered. BN: nodes WTI_Regime, VIX_Level, DXY_Trend, IndPro_Trend, CPI_Trend (train-tertile discretisation), hill climbing with BIC/K2/BDeu, max 3 parents, Laplace α=1 CPDs, exact inference. Selection on validation accuracy (tie-break log-loss); test evaluated once. Baselines: majority, persistence, random. Statistics: Wilson CI, exact binomial, exact McNemar, KS, Mann–Whitney, Fisher exact. Differences from Alvi (2018) and the notebook are in the app's *Validation & Replication* page.
Computed results (default run): validation 45.5% vs majority 50.0%; test 60.9% vs majority 87.0% (n=22/23). Variant V3 (previous-regime node) reaches 91.3% on test, essentially the persistence baseline (91.3%).
